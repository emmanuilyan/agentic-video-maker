## Spoken idea

The creator calls the technique “cutting the curve.” His instructions are to give the first object keyframes with heavy ease in and out, parent the second object to the first so it matches speed and position, and make a hard cut at peak velocity. He says the method can connect photos and scenes seamlessly. Browser-rendered English captions supported this summary at approximately 0:05–0:19.

## Reusable production decision

Keep a shared trajectory across the edit. The outgoing and incoming layers should align in screen position, direction, and approximate velocity at the cut frame; use a hard cut near maximum speed, with acceleration and deceleration on either side. Evaluate the result at normal playback speed and with adjacent frames around the cut. A generic crossfade is not what this video demonstrates.

Do not reproduce the tutorial's red callouts or After Effects interface when applying the underlying cut to a different story, unless the user explicitly requests the tutorial presentation.
