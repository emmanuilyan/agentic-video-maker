## Production decisions to reuse

1. Identify the represented material and its role in the story before picking texture or imperfections.
2. Keep material-bound texture attached to that material; reserve global optical finish for the complete composition.
3. Select stepped timing for handmade cutout motion, and preserve smooth movement when a camera glide helps the viewer inspect a screen or document.
4. Compare adjacent archive shots at delivery size and tune a shared treatment for continuity and readability.

These principles inform planning and critique. They are not approved effect implementations or presets for `remotion-photo-techniques`.
